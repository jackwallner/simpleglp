Keep your GLP-1 routine in one place. Simple GLP records the doses you log, shows your schedule and keeps a clear history for weekly shots and daily pills.

YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
Log a dose, add notes or symptoms, and review past entries. Record an earlier dose with its actual time, edit a mistake, or undo a recent log. Daily pill plans include an in-app wait countdown and an optional completion notification.

A CLEAR HISTORY
See your calendar, dose history and adherence patterns. Export your records as CSV or import a compatible Simple GLP export. Your records stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

REMINDERS YOU CONTROL
Set local reminders for the plan you enter. Android battery settings may delay a reminder. A reminder, schedule or countdown is a record-keeping aid, never a dosing recommendation.

OPTIONAL SIMPLE GLP PRO
Pro adds supply tracking and refill reminders, proactive alerts and an ongoing Android pill countdown notification. Logging, schedules, local reminders, history, the in-app pill timer and basic patterns remain free. Plans and any eligible trial are shown before purchase through Google Play.

MADE FOR ANDROID
Works without an account. Health data import, a watch app and home screen widgets are not included on Android. Health context from a compatible CSV remains in your local records.

Simple GLP is a medication tracker, not medical advice. Enter medication, dose, schedule and pill waiting instructions that match your prescription. Ask your prescriber or pharmacist about your treatment. Never change medication or dose based on this app.
