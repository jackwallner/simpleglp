Simple GLP is the fastest way to log a weekly GLP-1 shot, designed for people who just want the moment recorded without turning their treatment routine into a spreadsheet.

ONE TAP TO LOG
Open the app, tap the Home Screen widget, or raise your wrist with Apple Watch. Your shot is timestamped immediately. Add dose, injection site, notes, and symptoms later if you want.

A SCHEDULE THAT KNOWS YOUR WEEK
Set your medication, dose plan, preferred weekday, and time. Simple GLP figures out whether each shot was early, on-time, or late and reminds you ahead of the next one.

OPTIONAL HEALTHKIT CONTEXT
With your permission, Simple GLP can attach context from Apple Health (weight, glucose, activity, sleep, heart rate, workouts, water, caffeine, calories, and protein) to each shot. Read-only. Never written back.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No analytics. No data sold. Your shot history and any captured Health context stay on your device, shared between the app, widget, and watch through a secure App Group.

SIMPLE GLP PRO (OPTIONAL)
Pro adds pattern-based proactive alerts so timing drift gets caught early. Subscribe in-app. Cancel anytime in Settings.

NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE
Simple GLP does not provide medical advice, dosing instructions, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always follow your prescriber's guidance.

Subscription details
Simple GLP Pro is available as an auto-renewing subscription (Monthly or Yearly, both with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
