Simple GLP is the fastest, most private way to log a weekly GLP-1 shot. It's free, needs no account, and takes nothing to set up. Built for people who just want the moment recorded, not another subscription or a spreadsheet.

ONE TAP TO LOG
Open the app, tap the Home Screen widget, or raise your wrist with Apple Watch. Your shot is timestamped instantly. Add dose, injection site, notes, and symptoms later if you want.

FREE, WITH NO ACCOUNT
Core logging is free and stays free. No sign-up, no login, no email. Open the app and log. That's it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No analytics. No data sold. Your shot history and any captured Health context stay on your device, shared between the app, widget, and watch through a secure App Group.

A SCHEDULE THAT KNOWS YOUR WEEK
Set your medication, dose plan, preferred weekday, and time. Simple GLP figures out whether each shot was early, on-time, or late and reminds you ahead of the next one.

OPTIONAL HEALTHKIT CONTEXT
With your permission, Simple GLP can attach context from Apple Health (weight, glucose, activity, sleep, heart rate, workouts, water, caffeine, calories, and protein) to each shot. Read-only. Never written back.

SIMPLE GLP PRO (OPTIONAL)
Everything you need to log is free. Pro is an optional upgrade that adds pattern-based proactive alerts so timing drift gets caught early. Subscribe in-app. Cancel anytime in Settings.

NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE
Simple GLP does not provide medical advice, dosing instructions, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always follow your prescriber's guidance.

Subscription Details
Simple GLP Pro is available as an auto-renewing subscription (Monthly $2.99 / Yearly $19.99 each with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase ($24.99). Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
