Simple GLP logs your GLP-1 doses in one tap: your weekly shot or daily pill, the injection site, how you feel, and notes. Free core features, no account required. Works on iPhone, Apple Watch, and widgets.

LOG A DOSE (FREE)
• Logs a dose with the time in one tap from the app, Siri, a widget, or Apple Watch
• Records the dose, injection site, and notes for each shot
• Rates nausea, appetite, food noise, and wellbeing from 0 to 5

INJECTION SITE ROTATION (FREE)
• Records each shot in the abdomen, a thigh, or an arm, left or right
• Shows your last injection site next to your next dose

REMINDERS (FREE)
• Reminds you on shot day at the time you choose, up to 3 hours early
• Repeats every 1 to 12 weeks or every 1 to 90 days
• Logs the dose from the notification with Took it, or snoozes it
• Skips the reminder when you already logged that dose

DAILY PILL WAIT TIMER (FREE)
• Starts a countdown when you log your pill, set to match your label or prescriber
• Notifies you when the wait is over

APPLE WATCH AND WIDGETS (FREE)
• Logs doses from the Apple Watch app, even away from your iPhone
• Logs a dose from the Home Screen widget in one tap, then opens the app to save it
• Shows your next dose or pill countdown on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

NO ACCOUNT, ON YOUR DEVICE (FREE)
• Opens straight to logging with no sign-up, login, or email
• Keeps your log on your device
• Shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics
• Exports and imports your history as CSV

MEDICATIONS
• Works with weekly semaglutide or tirzepatide shots, brand-name or compounded, daily GLP-1 pills, and any medication you add yourself

APPLE HEALTH (OPTIONAL)
• Reads weight, glucose, sleep, and more to show beside each dose, with your permission (read-only)

FREE / PRO
• Free: everything above
• Pro adds the wait countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, refill reminders, a missed-dose reminder, and alerts when shot timing drifts
• Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew unless canceled 24 hours before the period ends.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
Simple GLP records what you tell it you took and when. It gives no medical advice, dosing instructions, or diagnosis. Follow your prescriber's guidance.
