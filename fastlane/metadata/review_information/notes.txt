Simple GLP is a private, on-device GLP-1 shot tracker. No account or sign-in exists — the app launches straight into onboarding, so the demo credential fields are intentionally blank.

CORE FLOW
1. Launch → 5-step onboarding (welcome, medication, schedule, reminders, Health context). The HealthKit read prompt and the notification prompt are both optional and may be declined; the app works fully without them.
2. The first tab is named "One Tap". Tap the large green "I took my shot" button to log a shot. A confirmation appears with an "Undo" option.
3. "History" tab lists logged shots (tap a row to edit details; swipe to delete, with confirmation). "Patterns" tab shows adherence/timing stats once 5 shots are logged.
4. The Home Screen widget (small/medium) and the Apple Watch app offer the same one-tap log via App Intents and WatchConnectivity.

SUBSCRIPTION (Simple GLP Pro — optional, never required)
Products: yearly and monthly auto-renewing subscriptions plus a lifetime one-time purchase, served via RevenueCat.
Where to find the paywall:
- Settings tab → "Upgrade to Pro", or
- Patterns tab → tap the blurred "What Pro watches for you" preview or the "See Plans" button.
- A dismissible offer sheet also appears once after onboarding and once after the first logged shot (close via the X button).
Pro unlocks "Proactive alerts" (Settings → Pro section after purchase): pattern-based dose-day notifications and a late-dose nudge. Price, renewal term, free-trial language (shown only to trial-eligible Apple IDs), Restore Purchases, Terms (standard EULA), and Privacy Policy links are all on the paywall. Restore Purchases is also in Settings → Pro.

DATA & PRIVACY
All shot data stays on device (SwiftData in an App Group). HealthKit is read-only context attached to logged shots; nothing is written back and nothing leaves the device. Settings → Data offers CSV "Export" and "Import". No analytics SDKs; RevenueCat processes purchases only.

The app does not provide medical advice or dosing instructions; a disclaimer is shown in Settings → About. "GLP-1" refers to the medication category the user is prescribed; the app records timestamps and user-entered context only.
