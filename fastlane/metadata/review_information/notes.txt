Simple GLP is a private, on-device tracker for GLP-1 medication: weekly shots and, new in 1.2.0, daily pills. No account or sign-in exists; the app launches straight into onboarding, so the demo credential fields are intentionally blank.

CORE FLOW
1. Launch → onboarding (welcome, medication, schedule, reminders, Health context, optional Pro offer). On the medication step, choose "Shot" or "Pill". The HealthKit read prompt and the notification prompt are optional and may be declined; the app works fully without them.
2. The first tab is "One Tap". For a shot plan, tap the large green "I took my shot" button. A confirmation appears with an "Undo" option.
3. NEW, daily pills: pick "Pill" in onboarding (Wegovy pill, Rybelsus, Foundayo, or Other pill) or change the plan later in Settings → Medication & schedule. "I took my pill" logs the dose and starts a countdown whose length the user sets (the "Wait before food & drink" stepper, 0-120 min). The app sends a local notification when it ends. The same countdown shows on the Home Screen widget and the Apple Watch app. The wait length is a user setting to match their own prescriber's instructions; the app does not recommend it.
4. "History" lists logged doses (tap to edit; swipe to delete with confirmation). "Patterns" shows adherence and timing stats once 5 doses are logged.
5. The Home Screen widget (small/medium) and the Apple Watch app offer the same one-tap log via App Intents and WatchConnectivity.

SUBSCRIPTION (Simple GLP Pro, optional, never required)
Products: yearly and monthly auto-renewing subscriptions plus a lifetime one-time purchase, served via RevenueCat.
Where to find the paywall:
- Settings tab → "Upgrade to Pro" or "Supply & refills" (PRO badge), or
- Patterns tab → the blurred Pro preview or "See Plans", or
- on a pill plan, the "Put this countdown on your Lock Screen" row under a running countdown.
- A dismissible offer sheet also appears once after onboarding and once after the first logged dose.
Pro unlocks: the pill countdown as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (starts when a pill is logged in the app), supply tracking with a refill reminder (Settings → Supply & refills), and Proactive alerts (late-dose nudge, timing patterns). Price, renewal term, free-trial language (only for trial-eligible Apple IDs), Restore Purchases, Terms (standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links are on the paywall. Restore Purchases is also in Settings → Pro.

DATA & PRIVACY
All data stays on device (SwiftData in an App Group). HealthKit is read-only context attached to logged doses; nothing is written back and nothing leaves the device. Settings → Data offers CSV Export and Import. No analytics SDKs; RevenueCat processes purchases only.

The app does not provide medical advice or dosing instructions; a disclaimer is shown in Settings → About. It records what the user says they took and when. Dose strengths and the wait length are only picker defaults the user sets to match their prescriber.
