## Decisions

- Kept the US, Canada, and Australia names and subtitles because current positions support their existing words. The US listing is #1 for `simple glp`, #10 for `glp-1 dose tracker`, #13 for `foundayo`, and #15 for `dose tracker`. Canada is #1 for `simple glp`, #2 for `foundayo`, and #5 for `glp-1 dose tracker`.
- Kept the Turkish title `Simple GLP - GLP-1 Günlük` because it ranks #1 for `simple glp günlük` and #3 for `glp-1 günlük`; the more grammatical `glp-1 günlüğü` query ranks #17.
- Changed the Turkish subtitle from `İğne ve hap günlüğü, özel` to the more natural `Özel iğne ve hap günlüğü`. It retains the same words. The pre-upload ranks include `haftalık iğne` #5, `özel iğne` #1, and `iğne günlüğü` #13.
- The June 26 ASO plan predates the September 23, 2026 store copy in commit `f754ead5`. The older plan's proposed title and subtitle are stale. The current `Dose Tracker` title and `Wegovy Pill & Weekly Shot Log` subtitle are supported by current ranks. The older US claim `private glp tracker` #97 no longer holds; the app was not returned in 193 results. Australia ranks #30 for that query, so `private` stays in en-AU.
- Keyword-only changes are bounded to weak terms, duplicate tokens, and local query gaps. `en-CA` keywords stay unchanged because `oral semaglutide` is #41 there. `en-AU` keeps `semaglutide` because `compounded semaglutide` is #15.
