## Can I log my GLP-1 dose from my Apple Watch or a Home Screen widget?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 29, 2026

**Yes. [Simple GLP: GLP-1 Dose Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simple-glp-glp-1-dose-tracker/id6770137909?pt=128748779&ct=jw-simpleglp-log-glp-1-dose-apple-watch-&mt=8) logs a weekly GLP-1 shot or daily pill in one tap from its Apple Watch app or its Home Screen widget.** Both are free and need no account. The widget and the Watch also show your next dose, and for a daily pill, the wait countdown.

## How do I log from the Home Screen widget?

1. Add the Simple GLP widget to your Home Screen in the small or medium size.
2. Tap the log button on the widget. The dose is stamped with the time of your tap, and the app opens to finish saving it.
3. The widget then shows your next dose. For a daily pill it shows the wait countdown that the log started.

## How do I log from Apple Watch?

1. Open Simple GLP on your Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later).
2. Tap the log button. The Watch confirms the dose is logged and shows your next dose, or for a pill, how long until the wait ends.
3. The log is sent to your iPhone. If the iPhone is out of reach, the Watch keeps the log and it syncs when they reconnect.

## What gets saved with a one-tap log?

The time, the dose from your plan, and whether the dose was early, on time or late against your schedule. Open the app later to add an injection site, notes or how you felt. Siri and the reminder's Took it button check first and do not log the same dose twice.

## What can't it do yet?

- There is no Apple Watch face complication. Use the Watch app, or the reminder notification on your wrist.
- The Lock Screen widget only shows your schedule and wait. To log, use the Home Screen widget, the Watch or Siri.
- Your log stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, with no sync to other devices.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is logging from the widget and Apple Watch free?

Yes. One-tap logging from the widget, Apple Watch, Siri and the reminder is free with no account.

### Does the Apple Watch app need my iPhone nearby?

No. You can log on the Watch, and the dose reaches your iPhone the next time they connect.

### Which iPhone and Watch versions does Simple GLP need?

iOS 17 or later on iPhone, which also covers the interactive widget, and watchOS 10 or later on Apple Watch.

### Can I log a dose I took earlier?

Yes. In the app you can log a dose at the time you actually took it, or a missed day, and edit entries later.

### Does Simple GLP write my doses to Apple Health?

No. It keeps its own log. Apple Health access is optional and read-only, used to show context such as weight or sleep next to your doses.
