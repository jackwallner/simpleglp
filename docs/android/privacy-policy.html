Simple GLP Android Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026

Your medication records stay on your device

Simple GLP works without an account. Your medication plan, doses, notes, symptoms, imported health context, supply entries, reminders and preferences are stored locally. We do not receive those records, send them to RevenueCat or use them for advertising. Android does not import Apple Health data.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and entitlement information, and limited app and device context to validate purchases, deliver the paid features and restore access. We do not send RevenueCat medication names, doses, notes, symptoms or your CSV files. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications and exports

Reminders and countdown notifications are scheduled on your device. The ongoing pill countdown uses a generic timer on the Lock Screen. You choose whether to allow notifications. CSV exports leave the app only when you choose a destination or share them. Anyone with access to your exported file may see its contents.

Retention and deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes the plan, history and preferences stored by Simple GLP on this device. This does not cancel a subscription or erase transaction records held by Google Play and RevenueCat. To request deletion of purchase or other data held for us, email jackwallner@gmail.com and say that your request concerns Simple GLP on Android. We will help identify the relevant anonymous purchase records. Records needed for billing, legal obligations or fraud prevention may be retained. Your app does not create an account.

Support and children

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include. Avoid sending medication records unless needed for your request. Simple GLP on Android is intended for adults. It has no advertising SDK, advertising identifier collection, location tracking, contacts access or microphone access.

Contact

Email jackwallner@gmail.com for privacy questions. We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
