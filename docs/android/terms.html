Simple GLP Android Terms

Updated October 18, 2026

Tracking, not medical advice

Simple GLP records what you enter. It does not prescribe medication, recommend doses, diagnose or treat a condition. Enter the medication, dose, schedule and pill waiting instructions supplied by your prescriber or pharmacist. Default picker values are not instructions. Never change medication or dose based on the app. Reminders and Android notifications can be delayed or unavailable.

Your records

You are responsible for checking entries and keeping any backup you need. You can export your records as CSV. Deleting app data, removing the app or losing the device may remove local records. Apple Health, Apple Watch and iOS widgets are not included in this Android version.

Optional Simple GLP Pro

Core logging, schedules, local reminders, history, the in-app pill timer and basic patterns are free. Pro adds supply and refill tools, proactive alerts and the ongoing Android pill countdown notification. A lifetime purchase is a one-time unlock. Refund requests follow Google Play's rules. Restore purchases using the same Google account.
