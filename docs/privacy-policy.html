Simple GLP Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Simple GLP is a personal medication and shot tracking app for iPhone, Apple Watch, and widgets. It does not require an account or operate a server-side health database.

HealthKit data

With your permission, the app may read body mass, blood glucose, step count, active energy, exercise minutes, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, workouts, water, caffeine, dietary energy, and protein to show context around a logged shot. It does not write to Apple Health. HealthKit data is processed on-device and is not uploaded, sold, used for advertising, marketing, or data mining. Revoke access in iOS Settings.

Local storage

Shot events, medication plan details, schedules, notes, symptom ratings, optional Health context, settings, and reminder data are stored locally using SwiftData, UserDefaults, and an App Group shared with the Watch and widget workflows. Imports and exports are processed locally and shared only through the destination you choose.

Purchases

Simple GLP Pro purchases are processed by Apple and verified by RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, and limited technical context. It does not receive your shot history or HealthKit data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app, if available, or remove the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their policies. Exported files are controlled by the destination you choose.

Medical use and children

Simple GLP is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent, treat, or cure any condition. It is not medical, dosing, or emergency advice. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date above identifies the current version.
