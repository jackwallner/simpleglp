How are the dates worked out?

Each date is your last shot plus the number of days between shots, added again for every shot after that. On a 7-day schedule every shot lands on the same weekday. A shot on Wednesday, November 18 puts the next ones on November 25, December 2 and December 9.

Which medicines is this for?

Any injection taken on a fixed number of days. Ozempic and Wegovy and Mounjaro and Zepbound are once-weekly injections, so the calendar starts at 7 days. Use the interval your prescriber gave you.

What if a date has passed?

The calendar keeps counting from the date you entered and marks how long ago a date was. It does not say what to do about a late or missed shot. The instructions that came with your medicine cover that, and your prescriber or pharmacist can answer.

What does Add to calendar do?

It makes a calendar file on your device with one repeating all-day event, starting on your next shot day. Open the file to add it to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook. Nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work out my next GLP-1 shot day?

Add the number of days between shots to the date of your last shot. On a weekly schedule the next shot is 7 days later, on the same weekday.

Can I add my shot days to my phone's calendar?

Yes. Add to calendar downloads a calendar file with a repeating all-day event on your shot days. Opening it adds the event to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.

Does the calendar tell me what to do if I miss a shot?

No. It only counts days from the date you enter. The instructions that came with your medicine say what to do about a missed dose, and your prescriber or pharmacist can answer questions.

Does this page save what I enter?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored, and it is gone when you close the page.
