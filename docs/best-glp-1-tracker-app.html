Best GLP-1 Tracker App for iPhone

By Jack Wallner · October 12, 2026

Best for medication-level charts and a large user base: Shotsy. Best for food, protein and photo tracking alongside doses: MeAgain or GlucoPal. Best for check-ins, trial-data comparisons and supply tracking: Glapp. Best for logging a dose in one tap with no account: Simple GLP. The apps differ mostly in how much they track beyond the dose itself.

How we compared. We read each app's App Store listing, including its description and device list, and pulled rating, rating count and update date on October 12, 2026. This comparison is based on the listings and not on hands-on testing. Disclosure: Jack Wallner makes Simple GLP: GLP-1 Dose Tracker, so read its entry with that in mind.

General information, not medical advice. These apps record what you tell them. They do not give dosing instructions or treatment recommendations, and neither does this page. Follow your prescriber's guidance.

The apps, one by one

Shotsy GLP-1 Tracker

Shotsy is the most established dedicated GLP-1 tracker here. Its listing describes one-tap dose logging for injections and pills, several medications with separate schedules, estimated medication-level charts, side effects, weight, calories, protein and water from Apple Health, and iCloud sync across your devices. It is free to download with a subscription tier, so check what the free tier includes, and its listing shows iPhone only.

MeAgain: GLP-1 Tracker App

MeAgain adds a food log with photo, barcode and search, protein, fiber and water goals, a side-effect journal, progress photos and a home screen widget. It suits people who want one app for meals and doses. The listing says some features require a subscription, and that breadth means more to set up than a dose log needs.

Zepbound & Mounjaro Tracker (GlucoPal)

GlucoPal combines dose logging with an injection-site body map, weight trends, photo and barcode meal logging, side effects and progress photos. Its listing says no account is required and mentions private iCloud backup and sync. Estimated medication-level forecasts and PDF reports for appointments are in the optional Pro subscription.

Glapp: Smart GLP-1 Tracker

Glapp is the broadest in features. The listing describes a daily check-in for side effects, mood and sleep, a medication-level curve, a weekly progress report, comparison with clinical-trial data, nutrition tips and supply tracking that deducts each dose from your pens or vials. It also lists an Apple Watch app. It is the most to learn of the group.

Simple GLP: GLP-1 Dose Tracker

Jack Wallner's app. It logs a weekly shot or daily pill in one tap from the app, a Home Screen widget or Apple Watch, with reminders, history, a pill wait timer, an injection-site note and optional Apple Health context, all free and on your device. Pro adds Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdowns, supply tracking and missed-dose alerts. Limits: no food log, no progress photos, no medication-level charts, and no iCloud sync, so your history lives on the device. It has 1 rating, so its score says little yet.

Which should you pick?

  • You want estimated medication-level charts and the biggest user base: Shotsy.
  • You want meals, protein and photos in the same app as your doses: MeAgain or GlucoPal.
  • You want daily check-ins, trial-data comparisons and pen or vial tracking: Glapp.
  • You want a fast dose log on your wrist and widget, with no account and nothing else to maintain: Simple GLP.
  • You want your history synced between devices: choose Shotsy or GlucoPal, whose listings mention iCloud sync. Simple GLP keeps its log on one device.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best GLP-1 tracker app for iPhone?

It depends on what you want to track. Shotsy is the most established dose tracker, MeAgain and GlucoPal add food and photos, Glapp adds check-ins and supply tracking, and Simple GLP is a minimal one-tap dose log with Apple Watch and widget logging.

Do GLP-1 tracker apps give dosing advice?

No. These apps record doses and reminders you set. Dosing and treatment decisions belong to your prescriber, and the listings for these apps say as much.

Do I need an account for a GLP-1 tracker?

It varies. GlucoPal says no account is required, and Simple GLP needs none. The other listings do not say, so check before you install.

Are GLP-1 tracker apps free?

All five are free to download. Each has an optional paid tier, and what stays free varies by app. Prices vary by region and change, so check the in-app price before you buy.
