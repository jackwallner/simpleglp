More info

How Simple GLP is built and what it is not.

The Pitch

GLP-1 treatment already has enough friction. Simple GLP focuses on one practical job: log the shot now, enrich it later if useful.

It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, dosing instructions, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

Simple GLP Pro

An optional upgrade for people who want to stay ahead of their schedule. Monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase is available, with a 7-day free trial on subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Dose-day nudges. If a planned dose hasn't been logged a few hours after it was due, Pro reminds you before the day fully slips.

Drift alerts. Pro watches your logged timing and flags when your usual dose day starts to shift, so you can course-correct early.

Everything else — logging, schedule, history, insights, HealthKit context, widget, and Apple Watch capture — stays free, forever.

What Mattered

One tap, no app launch. The Home Screen widget logs a shot directly through an interactive App Intent — no need to open the app first.

Free core, paid depth. Logging, schedule, history, insights, and HealthKit context are free forever. Pro is reserved for proactive nudges only, not gated basics.

Health context is optional, not required. HealthKit access is read-only and can be skipped entirely; the core logging flow never depends on it.

Privacy as default. No accounts, no third-party analytics or ad SDKs. The only network activity is App Store purchase validation for Pro. Shot history lives in SwiftData behind an App Group, shared only with the widget and watch app.
