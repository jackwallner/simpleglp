Weight Loss Percentage Calculator

By Jack Wallner. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Enter your starting weight and your weight today. The calculator shows the percent of your starting weight you have lost and the amount in pounds or kilograms. Add a goal weight to see how far along you are.

What is the formula?

Starting weight minus current weight, divided by starting weight, times 100. From 240 lb to 220 lb is 20 lb lost, and 20 divided by 240 is 8.3 percent. The answer is the same in pounds or kilograms, as long as both weights use the same unit.

Why use a percentage?

A percentage puts the change in proportion to where you started: 20 lb is 8.3 percent of 240 lb and 13.3 percent of 150 lb. Studies of weight management medicines report results the same way, as percent change from starting weight.

How is progress to a goal worked out?

The amount lost so far divided by the total you set out to lose. Starting at 240 lb with a goal of 180 lb is 60 lb in all. At 220 lb you have lost 20 of them, which is 33 percent of the way, with 40 lb to go.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my weight loss percentage?

Subtract your current weight from your starting weight, divide by your starting weight and multiply by 100. From 240 lb to 220 lb is 20 divided by 240, or 8.3 percent.

How many pounds is 5 percent of my body weight?

Multiply your starting weight by 0.05. That is 12 lb at 240 lb, 10 lb at 200 lb and 7.5 lb at 150 lb.

Is the percentage different in kilograms?

No. A percentage has no unit, so 100 kg to 90 kg and 220.5 lb to 198.4 lb both come to 10 percent.

Does this page save what I enter?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored, and it is gone when you close the page.
