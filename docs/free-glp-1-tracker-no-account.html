Is there a free GLP-1 tracker that needs no account and keeps my data private?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 29, 2026

Yes. Simple GLP: GLP-1 Dose Tracker is free on iPhone and Apple Watch and needs no account. Open it, pick your medication and start logging. Logging, reminders, history, Insights, the Home Screen widget, Apple Watch logging and the pill wait timer are free. Your dose history is stored on your iPhone, with no ads and no third-party analytics.

Where does Simple GLP keep my data?

  • On your iPhone. Your dose log lives in the app's local storage, shared only with its own widget and Apple Watch app.
  • No account. There is no sign-up, email or profile, so there is no server copy of your log.
  • No ads and no third-party analytics. Your data is never sold.
  • Apple Health is optional and read-only. If you allow it, the app reads context such as weight, glucose, sleep and activity to show next to your doses. It never writes to Apple Health, and the app works fully without it.
  • Purchases only. Pro purchases go through Apple and are verified by RevenueCat, which never receives your dose history or Health data. See the privacy policy.

What does Simple GLP track?

Weekly shots (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, or your own) and daily pills (the Wegovy pill, Rybelsus, Foundayo, or your own). Each log keeps the time, the dose from your plan and whether it was early, on time or late. You can add an injection site, notes, and how you felt (nausea, appetite, food noise, overall wellbeing).

What are the limits?

  • No sync between devices. Your log stays on one iPhone and its paired Apple Watch, so export a CSV now and then if you want your own copy.
  • No food log, weight goals, progress photos or medication-level charts.
  • It keeps a log. It does not tell you what dose to take or when.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Simple GLP?

No. There is no sign-up, email or login. You choose your medication and schedule on first launch and start logging.

Is Simple GLP really free?

Yes. It is free to download, and logging, reminders, history, Insights, the widget, Apple Watch logging and the pill wait timer stay free. Pro is optional and adds the Lock Screen countdown, supply tracking and extra alerts.

Does Simple GLP sell or share my health data?

No. There are no ads and no third-party analytics, and your dose history stays on your device. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can I back up or move my GLP-1 log?

Yes. Settings has Export, which saves every logged dose as a CSV file you can keep or share, and Import, which brings a CSV back in.

Does Simple GLP work without Apple Health?

Yes. Health access is optional and read-only, and you can skip it entirely.
