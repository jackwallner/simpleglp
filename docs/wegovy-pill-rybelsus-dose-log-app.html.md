## Is there a daily dose log app for the Wegovy pill and Rybelsus?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

**[Simple GLP: GLP-1 Dose Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simple-glp-glp-1-dose-tracker/id6770137909?pt=128748779&ct=jw-simpleglp-wegovy-pill-rybelsus-log&mt=8) logs your daily Wegovy pill or Rybelsus in one tap and keeps a streak and a month view of the days you logged.** It can also start a wait timer when you log, set to match your label or prescriber. It is free on iPhone and Apple Watch and needs no account.

## How does the daily pill log work?

1. Choose your pill when you set up: the Wegovy pill, Rybelsus, Foundayo or your own.
2. Set your daily reminder time. The reminder has a Took it button that logs the pill.
3. Log from the app, the Home Screen widget, Apple Watch or Siri. The dose is stamped with the time of your tap.
4. Check the streak and the month view to see which days you logged.

## What does each log keep?

The time, the dose in mg from your plan, and whether the pill was on schedule, a little early or a little late. You can add notes and ratings from 0 to 5 for nausea, appetite, food noise and wellbeing. The dose picker lists the strengths each product is sold in, or you can enter a custom dose, and the app never suggests one.

## What about the wait after the pill?

Set Wait before food and drink to match your label or prescriber, from none up to 120 minutes. The field starts at 30 minutes for Rybelsus and the Wegovy pill and at none for Foundayo or a custom pill. Those are starting values only. When the wait ends, a notification arrives. The [pill wait timer page](https://jackwallner.com/ios/simpleglp/glp-1-pill-wait-timer-app.html) has the details.

## What are the limits?

- No sync between devices. Your log stays on one iPhone and its paired Apple Watch.
- No food log, weight goals, progress photos or medication-level charts.
- There is no Apple Watch face complication.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Simple GLP work for the Wegovy pill and Rybelsus?

Yes. Both are in the daily pill list, next to Foundayo and an Other pill option you can name yourself.

### Is the daily pill log free?

Yes. Logging, the daily reminder, the streak and month view, history and the wait timer are free with no account. Pro adds a second reminder, supply tracking and the live countdown on the Lock Screen.

### Can I log a pill I took earlier?

Yes. Log it at the time you actually took it, or log a missed day, and edit entries later.

### Does Simple GLP tell me how long to wait or what dose to take?

No. The wait length is a number you set from your label or prescriber, and the app records the dose you say you take.

### Does the daily reminder skip days I already logged?

Yes. If today's pill is already logged, that day's reminder is skipped.
