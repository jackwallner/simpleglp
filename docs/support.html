What Simple GLP Needs

Simple GLP can work as a manual shot log. Optional features may ask for:

  • Notifications, for reminders and proactive alerts.
  • HealthKit read access, for optional shot context like weight, glucose, activity, sleep, and nutrition.
  • Watch connectivity, for one-tap Apple Watch logging.

Subscriptions (Simple GLP Pro)

Simple GLP Pro is optional. Purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple ID.

  • To restore access on a new device, open Settings → Restore purchases in the app.
  • To change or cancel a subscription, open Settings → Manage subscription in the app, or go to Apple ID Subscriptions in Settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies.

Troubleshooting

  • If the widget or watch log does not appear in history, open the iPhone app once so it can process pending logs.
  • If Health context is missing, confirm Simple GLP has Health permissions in iOS Settings.
  • If reminders do not fire, confirm notifications are enabled for Simple GLP.
  • If purchases do not unlock, use Restore Purchases in Settings and confirm you are signed into the correct Apple ID.

Privacy

Simple GLP stores shot history locally and does not send HealthKit data to a server. Read the full Privacy Policy.
